Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026 · Operated by Cancer Treatment Specialist, PC

Our approach

Ask SHELIA is designed to work without an account. We don't ask for your name, email, or other personal identifiers, and we never sell your information.

Information you type into the chat

When you ask SHELIA a question, your message is sent to Microsoft Copilot Studio, the third-party AI service that generates SHELIA's replies. Microsoft processes it under its own privacy and security terms and may keep conversation transcripts for a limited time to operate, secure, and improve the service. Your messages are not linked to your name or an account by us.

Please don't include personal identifiers (such as your name, address, phone number, or medical record numbers) in your questions.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic, anonymous app usage and diagnostic information (such as which screens are opened and crash reports) to keep the app working.
  • Your agreement to the AI notice is saved only on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent information.
  • We do not use your chats for advertising or share them with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Not medical advice

SHELIA provides general health education only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe, and AI answers can be inaccurate. Always talk with your doctor about your health. In an emergency, call 911.

Age

Ask SHELIA is intended for adults 18 and older.

Links to other sites

SHELIA may link to outside health resources. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new effective date.
