Beat a craving in 5 minutes

Most cravings pass in 5–10 minutes. Ride the wave.
  1. DelayTell yourself "not for 10 minutes." Set a timer.
  2. Breathe deeplyBreathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 6. Repeat 5 times.
  3. Drink waterSip a full glass of cold water slowly.
  4. Do something elseTake a short walk, text a friend, or chew sugar-free gum.
  5. Log itMark your craving in today's check-in. Spotting your triggers makes the next one easier.

Cutting back on alcohol safely

If you drink heavily every day, talk with a doctor before you stop. Stopping suddenly can cause dangerous withdrawal, including shakes, confusion, or seizures. A doctor can help you cut back safely.

Quick answers

What if I slip up?

A slip is not failure. Most people who quit for good had slips along the way. Log it honestly, notice what triggered it, and start a new streak today.

Do quit medicines really help?

Yes. Nicotine patches, gum, lozenges, and prescription medicines can double your chances of quitting smoking. Ask your doctor or pharmacist which is right for you.

Is vaping safer than smoking?

Vapes still contain nicotine, which is addictive, plus other chemicals. If you vape to quit smoking, plan to stop vaping too. Free coaching above can help with both.

How much alcohol is "too much"?

Less is better for your health. If you drink, guidelines suggest no more than 1 drink a day for women or 2 for men. One drink is 12 oz of beer, 5 oz of wine, or 1.5 oz of liquor.
